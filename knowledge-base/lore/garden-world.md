# Garden World

Red is a bunny searching for Blue, another bunny who is hiding somewhere in the garden. The garden is a clue-based puzzle space where each solved puzzle moves Red closer to Blue.

The garden is divided into two main areas: the Outer Garden and the Inner Garden. The Outer Garden is the smaller starting area. The Inner Garden is beyond a locked gate and is three times larger than the Outer Garden.

Rock is placed in the Outer Garden. Red begins near Rock and the locked gate. Red cannot enter the Inner Garden until the gate is unlocked.

Blue's hiding place should not be revealed directly. The story should feel like a gentle garden search, where Red follows clues, notices patterns, and earns progress by solving puzzles.

The first major transition is from the Outer Garden into the Inner Garden. Unlocking the gate is Red's first step toward finding Blue.
