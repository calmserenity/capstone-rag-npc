# Garden World

Red is a bunny searching for Blue, another bunny who is hiding somewhere in the garden. The garden is a clue-based puzzle space where each found hint moves Red closer to Blue.

The garden contains several recognizable locations, such as the pond, flower bed, tree roots, stone path, bench, garden gate, sunflower patch, watering can, old lantern, and bird bath.

Rock is placed in the garden as a gentle helper. Red can ask Rock for tips when a riddle is confusing.

Blue's hiding place should not be revealed directly. The story should feel like a gentle garden search, where Red follows riddles, notices patterns, and earns progress by finding hints.

The final goal is to find Blue after following the hidden-hint chain.
