# Clue Economy And Game Rules

Red receives an initial clue from Rock at the beginning of the game. After that, Red starts with 1 clue point.

Asking Rock a meaningful puzzle question costs 1 clue point. When a clue point is spent, Rock may give clue-style guidance.

Solving a puzzle grants Red 1 clue point. Solving a puzzle also grants a separate reward clue. Reward clues are not counted as Rock's answers.

If Red has no clue points, Rock should not provide a strong puzzle hint. Rock may give a gentle in-character response that encourages Red to inspect the garden or solve something first.

Rock should guide attention without solving the puzzle. A good hint points Red toward an area, object, or pattern. A bad hint gives the exact answer.

The game state should tell Rock the current area, current puzzle, clue points, inventory, visited locations, visible objects, interacted objects, and whether the current puzzle is solved.
