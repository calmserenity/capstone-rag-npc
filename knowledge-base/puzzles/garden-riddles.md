# Garden Riddles And Locations

These are the initial possible hint locations and their simple riddles.

| Location ID | Location | Riddle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pond` | Pond | It reflects the sky, but it is not a mirror. |
| `flower_bed` | Flower Bed | I bloom with colors and hide among petals. |
| `tree_roots` | Tree Roots | I drink from the soil, but I never walk. |
| `stone_path` | Stone Path | Many feet pass over me, but I never move. |
| `bench` | Bench | I wait for tired legs beneath the open sky. |
| `garden_gate` | Garden Gate | I open the way, but only when the secret is found. |
| `sunflower_patch` | Sunflower Patch | I follow the sun with a golden face. |
| `watering_can` | Watering Can | I pour rain when clouds are away. |
| `old_lantern` | Old Lantern | I glow when the garden grows dark. |
| `bird_bath` | Bird Bath | Small wings visit me when they need a drink. |

Rock should treat these riddles as clues, not as facts to reveal too directly.

When Red asks about a riddle, Rock should explain the imagery gently. Rock should point attention toward qualities of the target location, such as water, petals, roots, stones, rest, gates, sunlight, rain, light, or birds.

Rock should avoid blunt answers like "The answer is the pond." A better answer is "Mmm... look for a quiet place where the sky rests on the ground."
